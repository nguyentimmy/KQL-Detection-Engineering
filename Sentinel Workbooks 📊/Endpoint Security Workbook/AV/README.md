## 🦠 AV Detections

Native antivirus hits across the fleet. The layer that shows what was caught and remediated — or wasn't.
Panels covering detections by threat name, by device, and by remediation status — designed to catch what didn't clean up, not to re-litigate what did.

🎯 Purpose

This section tracks AV detections estate-wide, with particular attention to anything that fired but wasn't successfully remediated — that's the gap between "Defender saw it" and "Defender actually stopped it."

It's a signal source, not a triage queue. An unremediated detection, or repeated detections on one device, becomes the trigger to check Endpoint Hunts or open Device Compromise Triage.
